# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial release of SomaBar, a SomaFM menu bar player for macOS.

- All SomaFM channels with search, local favorites, and recently played
- Now playing in the menu bar with ICY stream titles and album enrichment
- Four stream qualities (MP3 highest, AAC 128k, AAC+ 64k, AAC+ 32k)
- Self-healing streams with reconnect and sleep/wake recovery
- Global shortcuts, media keys, and macOS Now Playing integration
- Track change and channel switch notifications with channel artwork
- Listening history with liked/disliked songs and stats.fm export
- Last.fm scrobbling with an offline queue
- Sleep timer, output device picker with AirPlay, launch at login
- Sparkle automatic updates
